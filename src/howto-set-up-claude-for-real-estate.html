What you need before you start

  • A computer. Setup happens in the Claude desktop app on a Mac or Windows machine. After setup you can open your workbook on a phone anywhere Google Sheets or Excel runs, but the setup itself is a desktop job.
  • A paid Claude plan. The cards use Claude features that need one. Claude Pro is currently $20 a month, sold by Anthropic and not included in our price. The more cards you run and the more often they update, the more of your plan they use, so heavier use may need a higher plan.
  • Google Sheets or Excel. Either works. A free Google account is the easier path if you want your trackers to update on their own.
  • Your CRM, optional. If Claude has a connector for it, Claude can read it directly. If not, a spreadsheet export from the CRM does the job. Nothing depends on a connector.

What you do not need: any coding, any new software to learn, or a developer. You paste one prompt and answer plain questions.

Step 1. Sign up and get the app

  1. Go to claude.ai and sign up with your email or your Google account.
  2. Pick a paid plan. Pro works for most agents.
  3. Install the desktop app from claude.ai/download, open it and sign in. Claude is made by Anthropic; AiDealDeck is independent and is not made or endorsed by Anthropic.

Step 2. Give Claude a folder

Claude can read files in a folder you hand it. Start a new chat in the desktop app, click the + button and add your Downloads folder. That is where every card lands when you download it, so you only do this once.

Step 3. Paste a card

A card is one tool for one part of your business: your numbers, your listings, your new leads, your money or your assistant. Each one is a zip with a START HERE guide, a workbook, an example workbook filled with sample data, a copy-and-paste prompt and a Setup Pack that Claude reads.

  1. Save the card to your Downloads folder and unzip it.
  2. Open the file named COPY THIS PROMPT. Copy all of it.
  3. Paste it into the chat and press Enter.
  4. Claude reads the Setup Pack and asks you simple questions. Answer them. Approve what it sets up.

Setup time is different for everyone. Claude walks you through it one step at a time. The Lead-for-Life System takes the longest, because you build your follow-up plan in your CRM and write your own emails.

Start with the two free cards

The Listing Watchdog and the Morning Brief are free, and they are a good first run because they show the two things Claude is good at here: watching and summarizing.

  • Listing Watchdog. You name the people to watch: past clients, people who went quiet after a listing appointment, referrals. It tells you when one of them lists with another agent, and when that listing expires or comes back. It is for fixing your own sales process, not for contacting a seller who is listed with someone else; always confirm on your MLS.
  • Morning Brief. One short note each morning: what is due, who needs a call, what slipped. With the free cards it covers the Watchdog. With the full Deck it reads every card you have set up.

Three habits that make it work

  1. Approve, do not assume. Claude drafts messages for you to review and send. It never texts, calls or emails anyone on its own. Read what it sets up, and check important numbers before you rely on them; AI can make mistakes.
  2. Keep the numbers flowing. A tracker only helps if your numbers go into it. Ten minutes a week by hand, or let Claude update from your CRM or your sheet on a schedule you set.
  3. Keep your data yours. Everything runs in your own accounts: your Claude account, your Google or Excel files, your CRM. We never see your leads, clients or numbers.
