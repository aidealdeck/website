The basics

What is AiDealDeck?

AiDealDeck sells do-it-yourself AI toolkits for real estate pros. We call each toolkit a card. A card is a workbook, a short guide and one copy-and-paste prompt. You paste the prompt into Claude and Claude helps you set that tool up.

What is a card?

One tool for one part of your business: your numbers, your listings, your new leads, your money or your assistant. Each card comes with a blank workbook, a sample workbook, a START HERE guide, the setup prompt and a Setup Pack that Claude reads.

Are these real, physical cards? Does anything get mailed to me?

No. The cards are digital. Nothing is printed and nothing is mailed. We call them cards because each one is one tool. You get a download link by email right after checkout, and the files open on your computer.

What is The AI Agent Deck?

All five cards in one download, plus two bonus cards (Listing Watchdog and Morning Brief). It costs less than three single cards.

Who is it for?

Real estate pros who want their back office organized: anyone running leads, deals and commissions in a real estate sales business without hiring a developer or learning new software.

What does it really do, step by step?

1. You buy a card. The download link is emailed to you. 2. You open Claude on your computer and paste in the card's prompt. 3. Claude asks you a few simple questions about your business. 4. Claude helps you build the tool: a tracker, a plan or a dashboard that is yours to keep. 5. You use it every week, and you can ask Claude to update it any time. Each card does one job: your numbers, your listings, your leads, your money or your assistant.

The cards

What does the Real Estate KPI Tracker do?

It tracks lead to appointment set, held, signed, under contract and closed, with the real percentage at each step. It works out the weekly number you need for the goal you type in, and shows which lead sources produce closings. More about this card.

What does the KPI Tracker do that my CRM dashboard does not?

Most CRM dashboards show activity: calls, emails, tasks. The KPI Tracker shows conversion. It counts lead, appointment set, appointment held, signed, under contract and closed, with the real percentage at each step, works out the one weekly number to hit for the goal you type in, and shows which lead sources turn into closings. If Claude can read your CRM or a spreadsheet export from it, the numbers come across without retyping.

What does the Listing Exposure Playbook do?

It gives every listing the same 14-day launch plan, with fill-in templates and day-7 and day-14 seller reports. More about this card.

What does the Lead-for-Life System do?

It lays out a follow-up plan for every new lead until they book, keeps a daily call list, scores how fast leads get a first contact, and includes a TCPA checklist. More about this card.

What does the Commission & GCI Ledger do?

It records every closing with its commission, fees, referral fees and splits, and keeps team totals separate from your own income. It is a tracking tool, not accounting software. More about this card.

What does the VA Playbook & Scorecard do?

It gives your assistant a narrow role with three jobs, a daily routine, an end-of-day report and a weekly scorecard. More about this card.

What is the Listing Watchdog?

A bonus card. You tell it which people to watch: past clients, people who went quiet after an appointment, referrals. It tells you when one of them lists with another agent, or when that listing expires or comes back. Public listing sites can be late or wrong, so always confirm on your MLS.

Can I use the Listing Watchdog to win back a seller who listed with someone else?

No. Do not contact a seller while they are listed with another agent. That can break your MLS and association rules. The Watchdog is for learning. It shows who went quiet and who you lost, so you can fix that step in your sales process and lose fewer listings. If a listing ends and the seller is free again, follow your own rules for expired listings. Always check your own rules first.

What is the Morning Brief?

A bonus card. It is one short summary from Claude each morning of what is due, who needs a call and what slipped. It reads every card you have set up.

Are the bonus cards really free?

Yes. The Listing Watchdog and Morning Brief are a free download. They are also included in the Deck. They still need the Claude desktop app and a paid Claude plan.

We plan to release more. New cards are separate products, sold separately, and no release dates are promised.
