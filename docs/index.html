Straight answers

What does it really do, step by step?

1. You buy a card. The download link is emailed to you. 2. You open Claude on your computer and paste in the card's prompt. 3. Claude asks you a few simple questions about your business. 4. Claude helps you build the tool: a tracker, a plan or a dashboard that is yours to keep. 5. You use it every week, and you can ask Claude to update it any time. Each card does one job: your numbers, your listings, your leads, your money or your assistant.

Are these physical cards?

No. They are digital. Nothing is printed and nothing is mailed. You get a download link by email right after checkout.

Is it done for me?

No. It's a do-it-yourself toolkit. You paste one prompt into Claude, answer simple questions, and approve what it sets up. Setup time is different for everyone. The Lead-for-Life System takes the longest to build your follow-up plan in your CRM and write your own emails.

I only have Google Sheets or Excel. Do I have to have Claude?

Yes, you need both. Claude does the building: you paste the card into Claude and it sets up your tracker in Google Sheets or Excel. Without Claude the card cannot build anything.

What else do I need?

The Claude desktop app and a paid Claude plan (Claude Pro is currently $20/month), sold separately by Anthropic. The more cards you run and the more often they update, the more of your Claude plan they use, so you may need a higher plan as you grow. Google Sheets or Excel. A CRM is optional; if Claude has a connector for it, Claude can read it directly.

Will it contact my leads for me?

No. It drafts messages for you to review and send. It never texts, calls or emails anyone on its own.

Will it get me more deals?

We don't promise more leads, listings, closings or income. It shows you your numbers and what needs you; results depend on your work.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final because the files are delivered instantly and you keep them. Please read this page and the Terms before you buy. If a file is missing or won't open, email us and we'll fix it.

Do I get a spreadsheet or a dashboard?

Both are in the box and you choose. Every card comes with a workbook for Google Sheets or Excel. During setup Claude asks whether you want a live dashboard as your main view; if you say yes, Claude builds it for you inside Claude from your own numbers, and the workbook stays as the place your data lives. You can try a sample dashboard before you buy.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. Two cards are free: the Listing Watchdog and Morning Brief. There is also a live demo of the KPI dashboard with sample data.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. AiDealDeck is an independent product built to work with Claude. It is not made or endorsed by Anthropic.
