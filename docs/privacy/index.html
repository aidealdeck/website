Privacy

Effective November 25, 2026. This policy explains what information Ai Deal Deck, a brand of The Showing Partner LLC ("we", "us"), collects through aidealdeck.com, our Gumroad store and our emails, and what we do with it.

1. What we never see

Our products run in your own accounts: your Claude account, your Google or Excel files and your CRM. We do not have access to them, and we never see the leads, clients, numbers or messages you put into the tools.

2. What we collect

  • When you buy or download (through Gumroad). Gumroad processes your payment and shares your name, email address, the product, the price, your country and order details with us. We never receive your full card number.
  • When you subscribe or email us. Your email address and anything you choose to write to us.
  • When you answer our feedback form or the "How did you hear about us?" box at checkout. Your answers, the name of anyone you say referred you, and your email address if you give it. We use these to improve our products and to send referral and creator rewards.
  • When you call us. Our phone line is answered by an automated voice assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so we can answer you, follow up and improve our service. If you give your name, email address or phone number on a call, we keep it to reply to you. Our voice provider keeps call records for a limited time.
  • When you visit our pages. We use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook and Instagram). It uses cookies and similar technology to record visits, which pages you view, button clicks and purchases, along with technical data such as your browser, device and IP address, and sends that to Meta. We use this to measure our ads and to show ads to people who visited our pages. Our buy links also carry tags that record which of our pages and buttons, and which ad campaign, led to a purchase; these tags describe the visit, not you. We also use Google Analytics, a tool from Google LLC, which uses cookies to count visits and show us which pages and buttons people use, how long each section of a page is on screen, how far people scroll, and what people type into the search box on our Questions page (so we can add missing answers). We also use Microsoft Clarity, a tool from Microsoft Corporation, which records how visitors move through our pages (mouse movement, clicks, scrolling and which parts of a page are viewed) as heatmaps and session replays, so we can improve the pages. Clarity masks what is typed into form fields by default. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for how Microsoft handles this data. Gumroad's checkout pages may also use cookies; see Gumroad's privacy policy.
  • When you ask for the free cards on our site. We collect the email address you type, send it to our script on Google, and email you the two free cards once. We keep the address and the date in our records so we know who received the cards. We do not add you to a mailing list from this form.
  • Chat with Sarah. Sarah is an automated AI assistant, not a person. When you type a question in the chat, it is sent to our own script so it can be answered. To write a reply, your question and the last few messages of that chat may be sent to an AI service provider (xAI Corp.). When that is not available, the chat answers from our Questions page inside your browser. Questions the chat could not answer are counted in Google Analytics so we can add the missing answers. Please do not type private or payment details into the chat.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver what you bought and help with support requests.
  • To send product emails and, if you subscribed or bought from us, updates about new cards and products. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To measure and improve our ads and pages.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and prevent fraud.

4. Who we share it with

  • Gumroad, which runs our checkout, delivery and customer emails.
  • Meta, through the Meta Pixel, for ad measurement and advertising.
  • Google, through Google Analytics, for visit and usage statistics.
  • Microsoft, through Microsoft Clarity, for heatmaps and session replays of visits to our pages.
  • Service providers who help us run the business, only as needed to do that work.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some state privacy laws treat sharing browsing data with an ad platform like Meta for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing". You can opt out as described below.

5. Your choices

  • Opt out of ad tracking on our site. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, our pages do not load the Meta Pixel, Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, or adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.
  • Emails. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Access, correct or delete. Email support@aidealdeck.com to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing". We will respond within the time the law requires. We may need to keep some records, such as order records, for legal or tax reasons.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and email subscriber details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

7. Children

Our products are for working real estate professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
