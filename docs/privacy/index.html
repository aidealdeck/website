Privacy

Effective November 25, 2026. This policy explains what information AiDealDeck, a brand of The Showing Partner LLC, a Wisconsin company ("we", "us"), collects through aidealdeck.com, our Gumroad store, our phone line, our chat and our emails, and what we do with it. It is part of our Terms.

1. What we don't see

Our products run in your own accounts: your Claude account, your Google or Excel files and your CRM. We have no access to them, and we don't see the leads, clients, numbers or messages you put into the tools, unless you choose to send them to us (for example, a screenshot in a support email).

2. What we collect

When you buy (through Gumroad). Gumroad processes your payment and shares your name, email address, the product, the price, your country and order details with us. We never receive your full card number.

When you subscribe or email us. Your email address and anything you choose to write to us.

Feedback and "How did you hear about us?" Your answers, the name of anyone you say referred you, and your email address if you give it. We use these to improve our products and to send referral and creator rewards.

Free cards and card picks. The email address you type and the date. For free cards, we email them once and may send a few follow-up emails about the free cards and our paid cards. For card picks, we keep a scrambled (hashed) record so each email gets one prize.

Phone calls. Our phone line is answered by an AI voice assistant, and we tell you at the start of each call that it may be recorded. We keep the recording, the transcript and any name, email or phone number you give, so we can answer you, follow up and improve our service. Our voice provider, xAI Corp., processes calls for us. If we call or text you back, it is only about your request. If you ask, the assistant will always tell you it is an AI. We do not create voiceprints or use your voice to identify you.

Chat with Sarah. Sarah is an AI assistant, not a person. Your questions and the last few messages of the chat are sent to our own script and to our AI provider, xAI Corp., to write a reply. xAI states that it does not use data sent through its API to train its models, and that it keeps that data for 30 days to check for abuse. xAI processes chat messages and call audio for us as a service provider. When xAI is unavailable, the chat answers from our Questions page inside your browser. We count questions the chat could not answer, so we can add answers. Please don't type private or payment details into the chat.

When you visit our pages (only after you click Accept).

  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) records visits, pages viewed, button clicks and purchases, plus browser, device and IP address, and sends them to Meta. We use it to measure our ads and to show ads to people who visited our pages.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) counts visits and shows us which pages and buttons people use, how far they scroll, how long sections are on screen, and what people search for on our Questions page. We remove email addresses and phone numbers from searches before they are sent.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) records how visitors move through our pages (mouse movement, clicks, scrolling) as heatmaps and session replays. Clarity hides what you type into forms, and we hide the chat window from it. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Our buy links also carry tags that show which of our pages, buttons and ad campaigns led to a purchase. Gumroad's checkout may use its own cookies; see Gumroad's privacy policy.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver what you bought and answer support requests, calls and chats.
  • To send product emails and, if you subscribed, asked for free cards or bought from us, updates about new cards and products. Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe link and our mailing address.
  • To send referral and creator rewards.
  • To measure and improve our ads, pages, chat and products.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, enforce our Terms and prevent fraud, including unauthorized sharing or resale of our products.

4. Who we share it with

  • Gumroad, which runs our checkout, delivery and customer emails.
  • Meta, through the Meta Pixel, for ad measurement and advertising (only after you accept cookies).
  • Google, through Google Analytics, for visit statistics (only after you accept cookies), and Google Workspace, which hosts our email and the script that stores free-card emails.
  • Microsoft, through Microsoft Clarity, for heatmaps and session replays (only after you accept cookies).
  • xAI, to write chat replies and to answer, record and transcribe calls.
  • Other service providers who help us run the business, only as needed for that work.
  • Authorities or others, if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, such as enforcing our Terms.
  • A buyer of our business, if we sell or merge it. This policy would still apply to your information.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some state privacy laws treat sharing browsing data with an ad platform like Meta as a "sale" or "sharing". You can opt out as described below.

5. Your choices

  • Cookies. Our pages don't load the Meta Pixel, Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity until you click Accept on the cookie bar. Click Decline and they stay off. You can change your choice anytime with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of each page.
  • Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends this signal, we treat it as a Decline and an opt-out of any "sale" or "sharing".
  • Ads. You can also block cookies in your browser or adjust your ad preferences in Facebook or Instagram.
  • Emails. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Calls and chat. You can always email us instead of calling or chatting.
  • Access, correct or delete. Email support@aidealdeck.com to get a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or opt out of any "sale" or "sharing". We will confirm your identity first, usually by replying to the email on file, and respond within the time the law requires. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. We may keep some records, such as orders, when the law requires it.

7. How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards, such as trusted providers, strong passwords, two-factor sign-in and limited access to our accounts. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

8. Visitors outside the U.S.

Our products are made for U.S. real estate professionals, and we are based in Wisconsin, in the United States. Your information is stored and processed in the U.S.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, The Showing Partner LLC is responsible for your information. We use it to fulfill your purchase (contract), for our legitimate interests in running and improving our business, to meet legal duties, and, for cookies and marketing, with your consent. You can withdraw consent, object to processing, or ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your information by emailing us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Our products are for working real estate professionals aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think we have, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. If we make a material change to how we use information we already have, we will email buyers and subscribers before it takes effect.
